# DFIR Investigation Checklist

## Before investigation
- [ ] Confirm authorization, incident owner, scope, and objectives.
- [ ] Identify safety, privacy, and operational constraints.
- [ ] Record systems, time sources, and timezone assumptions.
- [ ] Plan evidence collection and retention.

## Evidence handling
- [ ] Assign case and evidence IDs.
- [ ] Record collector, source, date/time, and acquisition method.
- [ ] Preserve originals and create working copies where appropriate.
- [ ] Calculate and verify SHA-256 hashes.
- [ ] Record transfers and access in chain of custody.

## Examination
- [ ] Record tool names and versions.
- [ ] Preserve command history and relevant outputs.
- [ ] Normalize timestamps carefully.
- [ ] Correlate independent sources.
- [ ] Separate facts from assumptions.
- [ ] Record gaps and alternative explanations.

## Reporting and closure
- [ ] Document findings and confidence.
- [ ] Record authorized response and recovery actions.
- [ ] Provide prioritized recommendations.
- [ ] Store evidence securely according to policy.
- [ ] Complete lessons learned and closeout notes.
