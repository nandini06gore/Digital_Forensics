# Advanced DFIR Case Studies

    ## Purpose
    Practice complex, multi-source investigations using synthetic and approved training evidence.

    ## Learning outcomes
    - Explain the core concepts and terminology.
    - Identify relevant evidence sources and their limitations.
    - Perform the relevant tasks in a documented, authorized lab.
    - Preserve evidence integrity and record provenance.
    - Interpret findings, uncertainty, and alternative explanations.
    - Communicate results and practical recommendations.
