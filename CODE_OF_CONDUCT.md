# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent while giving direct technical feedback.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and legal boundaries.
- Help keep labs safe, reproducible, and accessible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, sharing private data, or encouraging unauthorized access or unsafe handling of evidence.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates these standards.
